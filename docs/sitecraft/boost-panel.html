Questions Patients Ask

Does a same-day crown last as long as a lab crown?

Yes, and this is probably the single most common question we get asked about the service, which makes complete sense because it does sound a little too convenient to be true when you first hear about it. The ceramic block we mill from is the same material category that an outside dental laboratory would be working with, so the durability profile is genuinely comparable, and in our own practice we have been tracking these restorations for years now and simply have not seen a meaningful difference in how long they last compared to the traditional two-visit route.
